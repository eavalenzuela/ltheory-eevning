# The game's own sounds, as script/soundgen makes them: each from the
# description on its line (ElevenLabs' text-to-sound-effects), written to
# resource/sound/made/<name>.ogg. Game/View/Audio says which is heard when.
#
#   name | seconds | once or loop | what it should sound like
#   name | seconds | join         | FIRST then SECOND
#
# A line changed does not change the sound: script/soundgen --force NAME
# makes it again (and it will not be the same sound twice). A join is
# FIRST up to its loudest moment, fading out over 'seconds' after it,
# with SECOND (any sound under resource/sound) begun at that moment.
#
# What was tried and not kept (the owner's ear, 2026-11-11): generated
# laser shots and warp-drive sounds. The engine's old ones are better,
# and are what the game plays (weapon/pulse/1, jumpdrive).

# Weapons, a shot each. (Lasers keep the engine's old sound.)
weapon/projectile1 | 1.2 | once | Single shot from a large spaceship autocannon heard from inside the hull: a heavy mechanical thud and muffled boom with the metallic clank of the breech, deep, punchy, dry, no music, no voice
weapon/projectile2 | 1.2 | once | Single shot from a spaceship artillery cannon heard from inside the hull: a deep concussive boom with a short metallic ring and recoil clunk, weighty, dry, no music, no voice
weapon/hybrid1     | 1.2 | once | Single shot from a magnetic railgun on a spaceship: a very short rising electric charge whine cut off by a hard supersonic crack and a metallic ring, powerful, dry, no music, no voice
weapon/missile1    | 1.6 | once | A missile launching from a spaceship launcher tube: a pneumatic clunk and then a roaring rocket whoosh that fades away quickly, no music, no voice

# What is hit, and what blows up.
impact/shield1     | 0.8 | once | An energy force field absorbing a weapon hit: a short electric zap with a shimmering resonant hum that fades fast, no music, no voice
impact/hull1       | 0.8 | once | A projectile slamming into thick starship hull plating, heard from inside the ship: a heavy metallic bang with a brief groan of stressed metal, no music, no voice
explosion/ship1    | 3.5 | once | A spaceship exploding, heard through the hull from some distance: a deep muffled boom, debris clattering, and a low rumbling tail, cinematic, no music, no voice

# A gate: the surge that builds, and then (the owner's mix) the engine's
# old jump sound for the clap, begun at the surge's peak.
gate/surge         | 3.5 | once | A stargate flinging a starship across space: a huge electrical surge building for half a second, a bright crackling burst of energy, then a rushing wind-tunnel whoosh that fades away, cinematic, no music, no voice
gate/jump          | 0.6 | join | gate/surge then warpnode/exit.ogg
