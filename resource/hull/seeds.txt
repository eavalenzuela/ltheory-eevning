# Hulls of the game drawn by the old generator, by seed.
#
# One hull to a line: its type, a seed, and a size from 1 to 8 (how many
# plates the generator grows: 3, 4, 5, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, and from size 4 up
# a broad deck under each); and after those, in any order, if they are
# wanted: a style, if it is not to be in the generator's own (stout,
# broad, tall, spear, slab, soft), and the word painted.
#
# A hull named here is drawn as that seed makes it, in place of the
# design drawn for it in Game/View/Hull.luau: the generator's own mesh
# in the generator's own bare metal, at the size the hull's class is
# (the size above is how much hull the generator grows, not how big the
# ship is: a frigate is a frigate's size whatever it is grown at). Its
# engines are let into the ends it has astern, and its hardpoints are
# put on by rule: on its own faces where one will take a gun's whole
# foot, and where none will, on seats let into the hull. With the word
# painted it is drawn as the game's own hulls are instead, in the paint
# of the design it takes the place of. A line that starts with # is not
# read.
#
# Find seeds with:   bin/launch hulls        (S goes round the styles,
#                                             F shows one as a ship)
#                    script/hulls seeds 1 40 2 bare frigate
# The types are in resource/script/Core/Data/Ships.luau: a hull is
# called <class>.<name>. A faction has one hull of each class, or, where
# its ships have been gone through one by one (the Talmar's, at the foot
# of this file), the hulls its line names in
# resource/script/Core/Data/Factions.luau: several of a class, or none.
# And there are anybody's (frigate.courier, frigate.prospector,
# barge.quarry, industrial.hauler, freighter.dray). The classes, with
# how big a ship of each is (its radius: half its length) and the
# Vessari hull of it:
#
#   frigate               42 m   frigate.javelin
#   destroyer             75 m   destroyer.halberd
#   cruiser              150 m   cruiser.cuirass
#   industrial           180 m   industrial.caravan
#   attackbattlecruiser  200 m   attackbattlecruiser.glaive
#   battlecruiser        300 m   battlecruiser.pavise
#   battleship           400 m   battleship.bastion
#   barge                160 m   (a faction's own: the Talmar's barge.anteater)
#   freighter            650 m   (a faction's own: the Talmar's freighter.rhino)
#   capitalindustrial    700 m   capitalindustrial.emporium
#   siegedreadnought     950 m   siegedreadnought.trebuchet
#   carrier             1000 m   carrier.citadel
#   dreadnought         1300 m   dreadnought.rampart
#   supercarrier        1300 m   supercarrier.stronghold
#
# (The names of the hulls from the attack battlecruiser on are first
# drafts, in resource/script/Core/Data/Factions.luau. Until a line here
# names one of those hulls it is drawn from a seed that follows from its
# name: a stand-in, with nothing meant by it.)
#
# If a seed has not the room for a hull's hardpoints even on seats, the
# game says so when it starts and draws the hull as it was.
#
# frigate.prospector   12   2
# frigate.courier       7   2   stout painted
