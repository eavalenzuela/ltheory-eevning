# The overview's signs: one white glyph for each kind of thing in space,
# tinted by the game for what it is to the pilot (the owner, 2026-11-23:
# "I do like this manner", 16 px "the best compromise of size and
# clarity"). Drawn by script/artgen glyphsheet, a sheet at a time, from
# the hull insignia (cache/artgen/hulls-insignia2.png) and, after the
# first, the first sheet as the set they belong to.
# A line is: key | what it is drawn as. "== name" starts a sheet (up to
# 9 a sheet of 3 by 3, up to 16 of 4 by 4). A key drawn again on a later
# sheet is redrawn: the latest is the one put in. A key with a "-" in it
# is one of several tries, put in only by a "=> key | try" line.
# The owner's picks: freighter B (the pyramid), fighter 3 for fighters,
# fighter 2 for bombers, ultracapital 3; the industrial kept; the tower
# like the kanji for middle.
